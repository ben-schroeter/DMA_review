Market Surveillance Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 ↗Adopted 20 Jun 2019Evidence: thin

Proposed 19 Dec 2017 and adopted 20 Jun 2019 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Aims to improve the internal market by strengthening market surveillance of products covered by EU harmonisation legislation, so that only compliant products that protect health, safety, consumers and the environment are sold. It requires certain products to have an EU-based responsible economic operator, sets out market surveillance authorities' powers, including for online sales, creates the Union Product Compliance Network and frames controls on imported products.

What we know so far

The Regulation strengthened checks on products sold in the EU, including a rule that every product must have an EU-based responsible company. A 2025 Commission review found no clear improvement in compliance: about one in ten checked products still fails the rules, and in an online check only a third of the EU contacts given could be confirmed. Meanwhile direct imports of cheap parcels tripled to 4.6 billion in 2024, and the full evaluation is still awaited.
