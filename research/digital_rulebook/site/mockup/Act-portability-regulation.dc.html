Portability Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2017/1128 ↗Adopted 14 Jun 2017Evidence: thin

Proposed 9 Dec 2015 and adopted 14 Jun 2017 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Ensures that consumers subscribing to online content services, such as streaming of films, music, sport or e-books, in their home Member State can access and use them when temporarily in another EU country. It requires providers of paid services to offer such cross-border portability at no extra cost and with the same content, and sets rules for verifying the subscriber's country of residence.

What we know so far

The Portability Regulation lets people use their paid streaming, music and gaming subscriptions when travelling in other EU countries. The Commission's 2022 review found it works well: about a third of subscribers had used their services abroad, few complained, and providers and rightholders reported only minor costs, though some services still cap how long you can use them abroad. No independent study has measured its economic effects.
