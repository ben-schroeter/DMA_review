General Product Safety Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2023/988 ↗Adopted 10 May 2023Evidence: thin

Proposed 30 Jun 2021 and adopted 10 May 2023 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Aims to ensure a high level of consumer protection while improving the functioning of the internal market. It sets essential safety rules for consumer products placed or made available on the market, both offline and online, where no more specific EU safety rules apply. It sets obligations for manufacturers, importers, distributors and online marketplaces, and provides for market surveillance and product recalls.

What we know so far

The General Product Safety Regulation, applied since December 2024, updated EU rules on unsafe consumer goods, especially those sold online. The Commission forecast it would cut harm from unsafe products by about EUR 5.5 billion over ten years. So far there are only activity figures - a record 4,671 safety alerts in 2025 and over 1,200 marketplaces registered - while consumer-group tests still find most products bought on Temu and Shein non-compliant; no study measures effects on injuries or costs.
