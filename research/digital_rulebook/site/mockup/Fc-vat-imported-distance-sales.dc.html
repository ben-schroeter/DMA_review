Digital Acquis / Forthcoming

VAT on platforms facilitating distance sales of imported goods (remaining part)

WithdrawnFinance2023/0158(CNS)

The part of a 2023 VAT proposal still pending after the rest was adopted: rules for online platforms that facilitate distance sales of goods imported from outside the EU, and special arrangements for declaring and paying import VAT.

Von der Leyen I Commission · part of the Digital Decade agenda

Where it stands

Proposal
May 2023Parliament position
Nov 2023Council positionTriloguesAgreement

Latest · November 2026

Parliament gave its opinion in November 2023 and again in July 2025 after being reconsulted. Part of the proposal has been adopted; the Commission's 2027 work programme lists the remaining part for withdrawal within six months unless the current Council discussions succeed.

What it would have done

  • VAT rules for taxable persons, such as online platforms, that facilitate distance sales of imported goods.
  • Changes to the special scheme for distance sales of imported goods and to the arrangements for import VAT.

What it would have changed in the Digital Acquis

  • Common VAT system Directive · would have been amended

The Commission proposal, Parliament's opinion under the consultation procedure, and the work programme that lists the proposal for withdrawal.
