Critical Raw Materials Act

Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 ↗Adopted 11 Apr 2024Evidence: thin

Proposed 16 Mar 2023 and adopted 11 Apr 2024 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Aims to secure the EU's access to a secure, resilient and sustainable supply of critical raw materials. It identifies critical and strategic raw materials, sets benchmarks for EU extraction, processing and recycling capacity, supports 'strategic projects' with faster permitting and access to finance, requires monitoring and stress tests of supply risks, and promotes circularity and the free movement of these materials in the internal market.

What we know so far

The Act set 2030 goals for the EU to mine 10%, process 40% and recycle 25% of the strategic raw materials it uses, and to rely on no single country for more than 65%. The Commission has designated 47 EU Strategic Projects with EUR 22.5 billion of planned investment, but the European Court of Auditors concluded in 2026 that the goals are unlikely to be met. No study has yet measured the Act's effect on supply security or prices.

Who it applies to

Size thresholds and exemptions: Risk-preparedness duties apply only to large companies (over 500 staff and EUR 150 million turnover) that use strategic raw materials.
