Digital Europe Programme

Regulation (EU) 2021/694 ↗Adopted 29 Apr 2021Evidence: thin

Proposed 6 Jun 2018 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) and adopted 29 Apr 2021 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Funds the digital transformation of Europe's economy, industry and society for 2021-2027, aiming to improve EU competitiveness, bridge the digital divide and reinforce strategic autonomy. It supports deployment in five areas: high performance computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and trust, advanced digital skills, and the best use of digital capacities and interoperability, including through European Digital Innovation Hubs. Its budget is EUR 7.588 billion.

What we know so far

Digital Europe (EUR 8.2bn, 2021-2027) funds supercomputers, AI and cybersecurity infrastructure, innovation hubs and advanced skills. The Commission's 2025 mid-term review shows outputs largely on track - the JUPITER exascale computer, over 150 innovation hubs serving 54,600 firms and 20,700 people trained - but effects on firms' productivity or digital uptake have not been measured, and no independent evaluation was found.
