Omnibus IV (small mid-caps and digital product compliance)

Agreed, awaiting adoptionCompetition & Single MarketCOM(2025) 501

A simplification package for small mid-cap companies and product rules: SME relief extended to firms with up to 750 employees, and paper requirements in product laws replaced by digital ones.

Von der Leyen II Commission · part of the Competitiveness · One Europe, One Market agenda

Latest · November 2026

A provisional agreement was reported on 9 June 2026, reportedly raising the small mid-cap threshold to fewer than 1,000 employees for the GDPR change. Parliament's plenary vote is indicatively set for 23 November 2026.

What it would do

  • Extends SME relief to small mid-caps, including a narrower GDPR duty to keep records of processing.
  • Digital-by-default compliance documents and declarations of conformity in product laws.
  • Common technical specifications when harmonised standards are late.

What it would change in the Digital Acquis

  • General Data Protection Regulation · record-keeping duty narrowed (Article 30)

What the omnibus would change, law by law

Provisions the proposal replaces, deletes or inserts in each law on our list: 42 replaced, 2 deleted, 16 inserted. Counted from the amending instructions of COM(2025) 501 to 504 (the Commission proposals; the agreed texts may differ).
