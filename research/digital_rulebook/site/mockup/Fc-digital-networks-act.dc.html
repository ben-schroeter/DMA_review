Digital Networks Act

In negotiationConnectivity2026/0013(COD)RegulationTarget for agreement: Q4 2027

Turns the European Electronic Communications Code into a directly applicable regulation and adds EU-level coordination of spectrum, a timetable for switching off copper networks and a lighter single authorisation for operators.

Von der Leyen II Commission · part of the Competitiveness · One Europe, One Market agenda

Latest · November 2026

Parliament has referred the proposal to its Industry committee (ITRE), with Michał Kobosko (Renew) as rapporteur; IMCO and LIBE give opinions. In Council, the Cyprus presidency presented a progress report to the Telecoms Council of 8–9 June 2026: delegations back the aims but several question the shift to EU-level spectrum coordination and the reliance on implementing acts. Neither institution has adopted a position yet.

What it would do

  • One regulation in place of the telecoms code and the BEREC Regulation.
  • EU-wide coordination of spectrum assignment and longer licence durations.
  • A planned switch-off of copper networks by 2030–35.
  • A single EU authorisation for operators active in several member states.
  • Keeps the open internet rules.

What it would change in the Digital Acquis

  • European Electronic Communications Code · repealed and replaced
  • Open Internet Access Regulation · amended
  • Radio Spectrum Decision · amended
  • ePrivacy Directive · amended
