Digital Acquis / Forthcoming

Digital Fairness Act

AnnouncedE-commerce & Consumer ProtectionCWP 2026Likely a regulation or amending directive

Planned consumer rules for online practices the current law handles poorly: dark patterns, addictive design, unfair personalisation, influencer marketing and subscription traps.

Von der Leyen II Commission · part of the Competitiveness · One Europe, One Market agenda

Where it stands

Announced inWork programme 2026
ConsultationJul – Oct 2025
Proposal expected11 Nov 2026 (indicative)

Latest · November 2026

The Commission's public consultation and call for evidence ran from 17 July to 24 October 2025 and drew 3,341 consultation replies and 4,325 responses to the call for evidence; the factual summary report followed in December 2025. The proposal is in the 2026 work programme for the fourth quarter of 2026, and the Commission's indicative agenda of 20 October 2026 schedules it for 11 November 2026, in a consumer package with a consumer enforcement initiative. No impact assessment has been published yet.

What it would do

  • Ban or restrict dark patterns and addictive design features.
  • Rules on personalised pricing and advertising based on vulnerabilities.
  • Easier cancellation of subscriptions and limits on auto-renewal traps.
  • Clearer duties for influencer marketing.
  • Possibly a shift of the burden of proof in digital cases.

What it would change in the Digital Acquis

  • Unfair Commercial Practices Directive · likely amended
  • Consumer Rights Directive · likely amended
  • Unfair Contract Terms Directive · likely amended
  • Digital Services Act · overlaps on dark patterns
