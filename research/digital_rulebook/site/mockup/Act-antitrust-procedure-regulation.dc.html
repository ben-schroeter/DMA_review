Antitrust Procedure Regulation

Regulation (EC) No 1/2003 ↗Adopted 16 Dec 2002Evidence: thin

Proposed 27 Sep 2000 and adopted 16 Dec 2002 under the Prodi Commission (Erkki Liikanen) · Part of the Lisbon agenda

What it does

Sets the procedure for applying the EU antitrust rules (Articles 101 and 102 TFEU). It abolished the system of notifying agreements to the Commission: firms must assess for themselves whether agreements meet the exemption conditions, while the Commission, national competition authorities and national courts apply the rules in parallel within a network. It gives the Commission investigative powers (requests for information, inspections, interviews) and the power to impose fines of up to 10% of worldwide turnover and commitments.

What we know so far

Regulation 1/2003 ended the notification of agreements to the Commission and shared enforcement with national authorities and courts. The Commission's 2024 evaluation judged the framework broadly effective, citing 225 Commission enforcement decisions and more than EUR 42 billion in fines over 20 years, but found procedures slow and less suited to digital markets; a revision is under way.
