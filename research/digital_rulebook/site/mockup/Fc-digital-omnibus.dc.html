Digital Omnibus (data, privacy, cybersecurity)

In negotiationData & PrivacyCOM(2025) 837

A package of amendments to the EU's data, privacy and cybersecurity rules, meant to cut compliance costs without lowering protection. The AI part was split off and adopted in July 2026.

Von der Leyen II Commission · part of the Competitiveness · One Europe, One Market agenda

Latest · November 2026

The Council agreed its negotiating mandate on 21 October 2026. Parliament's committees are still working through more than 1,750 amendments, so trilogues have not started. The separate Digital Omnibus on AI was adopted as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and entered into force on 27 July 2026.

What it would do

  • Folds the Data Governance Act, the Free Flow of Non-Personal Data Regulation and the Open Data Directive into the Data Act.
  • Changes the GDPR: definition of personal data, legitimate interest for AI training, fewer cookie banners via rules moved from the ePrivacy Directive.
  • A single EU entry point for reporting cybersecurity and data incidents.
  • Repeals the Platform-to-Business Regulation, whose rules overlap with the DMA and DSA.

What it would change in the Digital Acquis
