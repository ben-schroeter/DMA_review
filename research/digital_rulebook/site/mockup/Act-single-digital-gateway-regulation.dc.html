Single Digital Gateway Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2018/1724 ↗Adopted 2 Oct 2018Evidence: moderate

Proposed 2 May 2017 and adopted 2 Oct 2018 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Creates a single digital gateway, integrated into the Your Europe portal, giving citizens and businesses easy online access to information, procedures and assistance services on EU and national rules relevant to the internal market. It requires key administrative procedures to be available fully online to cross-border users, applies the 'once-only' principle for exchanging evidence, and collects user feedback on internal market obstacles.

What we know so far

The Single Digital Gateway promised to save businesses EUR 11-55 billion a year by putting EU and national information and procedures online. The Commission's 2023 report shows the Your Europe portal is heavily used (43 million visits in 2022) with over 90% satisfaction, but only about half of key procedures could be completed online by users from other EU countries. The promised savings have not been measured, and the 2025 evaluation was not found.
