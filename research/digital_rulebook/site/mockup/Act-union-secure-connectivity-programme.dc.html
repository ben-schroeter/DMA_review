Union Secure Connectivity Programme

Regulation (EU) 2023/588 ↗Adopted 15 Mar 2023Evidence: thin

Proposed 15 Feb 2022 and adopted 15 Mar 2023 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Establishes a programme to provide secure satellite communication services to government-authorised users in the EU through a multi-orbital connectivity system under civil control (known as IRIS²). It also aims to enable commercial services, including high-speed broadband, and to reduce communication dead zones. It sets the programme's objectives, funding rules and implementation, with a budget of EUR 1.65 billion for 2023-2027.

What we know so far

IRIS² is the EU's planned secure satellite communications network. The impact assessment promised initial government services by 2025 and full capacity by 2027 for about EUR 6 billion; by August 2026 the expected cost had risen to over EUR 15.6 billion and first launches had slipped to 2029. No services exist yet, so none of the promised security, broadband or innovation results can be measured.
