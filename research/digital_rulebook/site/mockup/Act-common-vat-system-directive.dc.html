Common VAT system Directive

Directive 2006/112/EC ↗Adopted 28 Nov 2006Evidence: moderate

Proposed 15 Apr 2004 under the Prodi Commission (Erkki Liikanen) and adopted 28 Nov 2006 under the first Barroso Commission (Viviane Reding) · Part of the Lisbon agenda

What it does

Establishes the common system of value added tax in the EU, a general consumption tax charged on goods and services in proportion to their price, with tax paid at each stage deductible. It defines taxable persons and transactions, the place of supply, the chargeable event and taxable amount, rates, exemptions, deduction rights and the obligations of taxable persons.

What we know so far

The VAT Directive sets the EU's common VAT rules, which have been updated for online sales. Since the 2021 e-commerce reform, VAT collected through the EU's one-stop-shop rose to EUR 33 billion in 2024, though this is gross collection rather than a measured net gain. Independent research using EU VAT changes shows price rises from VAT increases are passed on about twice as fully as VAT cuts; the 2025 digital VAT reform is forecast to raise EUR 135-177 billion over ten years.
