DLT Pilot Regime Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2022/858 ↗Adopted 30 May 2022Evidence: thin

Proposed 24 Sep 2020 and adopted 30 May 2022 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Creates a pilot regime allowing market infrastructures to trade and settle financial instruments using distributed ledger technology. It sets requirements for DLT multilateral trading facilities, settlement systems and combined trading and settlement systems, lets competent authorities grant specific permissions and temporary exemptions from existing financial rules under conditions, and provides for supervision and cooperation with ESMA.

What we know so far

The DLT Pilot Regime lets trading venues and securities depositories test blockchain technology under relaxed rules. By mid-2025 only three had been licensed and live trading was minimal; the EU securities regulator blamed tight size caps and practical barriers. The Commission proposed in December 2025 to make the regime permanent with much higher caps; no study has measured effects on costs or competition.
