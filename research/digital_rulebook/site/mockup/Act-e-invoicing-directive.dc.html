E-invoicing Directive

Directive 2014/55/EU ↗Adopted 16 Apr 2014Evidence: thin

Proposed 26 Jun 2013 and adopted 16 Apr 2014 under the second Barroso Commission (Neelie Kroes) · Part of the Europe 2020 agenda

What it does

Aims to facilitate the use of electronic invoices in public procurement across the EU. It provides for a European standard on electronic invoicing and requires Member States to ensure that contracting authorities and entities receive and process electronic invoices that comply with that standard, reducing barriers caused by differing national formats.

What we know so far

The Commission forecast that full e-invoicing in public procurement could save EUR 1.5-2.3 billion a year. Its 2024 evaluation found the directive did create a common European e-invoice standard accepted by almost all central governments, but uptake is high only where it is mandatory and the promised savings were never measured. No independent study of the directive's effects was found.
