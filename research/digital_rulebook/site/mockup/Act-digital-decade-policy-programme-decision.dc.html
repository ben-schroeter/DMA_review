Digital Decade Policy Programme Decision

Decision (EU) 2022/2481 ↗Adopted 14 Dec 2022Evidence: thin

Proposed 15 Sep 2021 and adopted 14 Dec 2022 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Establishes the Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030 to set a clear direction for the EU's digital transformation. It sets EU-level digital targets for 2030 on skills, infrastructure, business digitalisation and public services, measured by indicators. It creates a monitoring and cooperation mechanism between the Commission and Member States, including national roadmaps and annual reports, and a framework for multi-country projects.

What we know so far

The Digital Decade programme sets EU targets for 2030 on connectivity, skills, business digitalisation and public services, and makes the Commission report progress every year. Its own reports show basic 5G at 96.8% of households and online public services above 80%, but the EU is far off target on AI use by firms (about 20% vs 75%), basic digital skills (just over 60% vs 80%), fibre and chips. No study has assessed whether the programme's governance changed what Member States do.
