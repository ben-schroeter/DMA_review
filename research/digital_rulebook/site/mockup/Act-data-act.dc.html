Legal instruments / Data & Privacy

Data Act

Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 ↗Adopted 13 Dec 2023Evidence: too early to tell

Proposed 23 Feb 2022 and adopted 13 Dec 2023 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Sets harmonised rules on access to and use of data. It gives users of connected products and related services the right to access data they generate and share it with third parties, sets conditions for data holders making data available to recipients, and allows public bodies to request data in cases of exceptional need. It also makes switching between cloud and other data processing services easier and supports interoperability standards.

What we know so far

The Data Act gives users of connected devices and businesses rights to access and share data, and has applied since September 2025. The Commission forecast EUR 270 billion of extra GDP by 2028, but nothing has yet been measured, and economists at Bruegel, CERRE and universities argue the access rules are weak or complex. A 2025 survey found almost no German firms ready, and the Commission has already proposed amendments in its November 2025 Digital Omnibus.

Who it applies to

Size thresholds and exemptions: Micro and small manufacturers are exempt from the data-sharing duties for connected products, and some duties to provide data free of charge apply only to larger data holders.
