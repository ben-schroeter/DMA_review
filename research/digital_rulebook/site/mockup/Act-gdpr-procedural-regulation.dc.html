GDPR Procedural Regulation

2023/0202(COD) ↗Adopted 26 Nov 2025Evidence: too early to tell

Proposed 4 Jul 2023 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) and adopted 26 Nov 2025 under the second von der Leyen Commission (Henna Virkkunen) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Sets common procedural rules for how national data protection authorities handle complaints and investigations in cross-border cases under the GDPR. It harmonises the admissibility of complaints, the rights of complainants and companies under investigation, such as the right to be heard and access to the file, and cooperation and dispute resolution between authorities, to make enforcement faster and more consistent.

What we know so far

The GDPR Procedural Regulation was adopted without an impact assessment or monitoring indicators, aiming to speed up cross-border data protection cases that often take years. It only applies to cases opened from April 2027, so there is no evidence yet on whether it works. Regulators backed it, while privacy campaigners warned the final text may be too complex to deliver faster decisions.
