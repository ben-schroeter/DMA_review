Payment Services Regulation (PSR)

2023/0210(COD) ↗AgreedEvidence: too early to tell

Proposed 28 Jun 2023 by the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton); agreed, awaiting adoption in October 2026 · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Would set uniform, directly applicable rules for payment and e-money services across the EU, covering transparency, information requirements and the rights and obligations of providers and users. It would strengthen measures against payment fraud, including fraud-data sharing and IBAN-name verification, improve open banking by tackling obstacles to account data access, and give payment institutions fairer access to payment systems.

What we know so far

The Payment Services Regulation (shared impact assessment with PSD3) is meant to cut payment scams through payee name checks, fraud data sharing and refunds in some impersonation cases, and to improve open banking; the Commission put the targeted scam losses at about EUR 323 million a year (2020). A political agreement was reached in November 2025 but the rules do not yet apply; reported payment fraud in Europe has since risen to EUR 4.2 billion in 2024.
