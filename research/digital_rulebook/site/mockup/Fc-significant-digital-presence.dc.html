Digital Acquis / Forthcoming

Corporate taxation of a significant digital presence

WithdrawnFinance2018/0072(CNS)

Proposed in March 2018 as the long-term reform of corporate tax rules for the digital economy: a company could be taxed where it has a significant digital presence, measured by revenues, users or business contracts, even without a physical presence.

Juncker Commission · part of the Digital Single Market agenda

Where it stands

Proposal
Mar 2018Parliament position
Dec 2018Council positionTriloguesAgreement

Latest · November 2026

Parliament gave its opinion in December 2018; the file has not been discussed in Council since 2019. The Commission's 2027 work programme lists it for withdrawal within six months.

What it would have done

  • A taxable 'significant digital presence' based on revenues, number of users or online business contracts in a member state.
  • Rules for attributing profits to that digital presence.

What it would have changed in the Digital Acquis

  • No law in the current Digital Acquis · would have been a new instrument

The Commission proposal, Parliament's opinion under the consultation procedure, and the work programme that lists the proposal for withdrawal.
