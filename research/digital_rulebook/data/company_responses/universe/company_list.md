# Companies in the frequency study (by company type)

Fortune 100 (2026) and DAX, CAC 40, FTSE 100 and EURO STOXX 50 constituents headquartered in the EU-27, UK or US in one of the six company types, plus additions, after the project lead's decisions (company_types.json). Each company is listed under its main type (other types in 'Also'). **Reports** = first and last report year found (2000-2025); **n** = years with a report; **Gaps** = missing years after the first. Sources: SEC 10-K/20-F, ESEF (filings.xbrl.org), company IR sites (incl. EQS-hosted files), the AMF storage mechanism (info-financiere.gouv.fr), annualreports.com.
