# Scope decision, November 2026

**Scope statement.** Laws governing digital technologies, products, services, data, platforms, cybersecurity, electronic communications, AI, digital identity, digital content and digital infrastructure, including online offences and data protection by public authorities, together with the general economic laws (competition, consumer, tax, IP, company and product law) that set key rules for digital markets. Funding programmes, industrial and crisis instruments, and laws on the internal administration of public bodies are not included.

Agreed with the project lead on 14 November 2026. Effect: 105 adopted laws - 21 dropped + 16 added = 100.
