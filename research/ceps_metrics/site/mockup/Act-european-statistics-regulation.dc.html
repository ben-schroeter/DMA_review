European Statistics Regulation

Regulation (EC) 223/2009 ↗Adopted 11 Mar 2009Evidence: thin

Proposed 16 Oct 2007 and adopted 11 Mar 2009 under the first Barroso Commission (Viviane Reding) · Part of the Lisbon agenda

What it does

Establishes a legal framework for the development, production and dissemination of European statistics. It sets statistical principles such as professional independence, impartiality, reliability and statistical confidentiality, defines the European Statistical System of Eurostat and national statistical authorities, and governs the European statistical programme, data access and the protection of confidential data.

What we know so far

The 2009 regulation is the legal basis for EU official statistics and the independence of statistical offices. The EU's auditors (2012) and peer reviews (2013-2015) found the quality code only partly applied, with over 700 recommendations, many on independence. A 2024 amendment giving statisticians access to private data is forecast to bring EUR 1.5 billion a year of benefits for EUR 757 million of costs; it has not yet been evaluated.
