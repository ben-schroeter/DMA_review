Technology Transfer Block Exemption

Regulation (EU) 316/2014 ↗Adopted 21 Mar 2014Evidence: thin

Proposed 20 Feb 2013 and adopted 21 Mar 2014 under the second Barroso Commission (Neelie Kroes) · Part of the Europe 2020 agenda

What it does

Exempts certain technology transfer agreements, such as patent, know-how and software copyright licences between two undertakings, from the EU ban on anti-competitive agreements. The exemption applies where the parties' market shares stay below set thresholds and the agreement contains no listed hardcore restrictions, such as price-fixing or certain market-sharing; some other obligations are excluded from the exemption.

What we know so far

This competition-law safe harbour for technology licensing had no monitoring indicators. The Commission's 2024 evaluation, based mostly on stakeholder views, found it largely did its job of giving firms legal certainty, but had gaps on early-stage technologies and data licensing; its 2026 replacement is forecast to cut legal-assessment costs by 15-40%. No independent study measuring its effect on licensing or innovation was found.
