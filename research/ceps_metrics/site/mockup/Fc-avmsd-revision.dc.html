Revision of the Audiovisual Media Services Directive

AnnouncedE-commerce & Consumer ProtectionEvaluation

A possible revision of the Audiovisual Media Services Directive after its evaluation, covering influencers, video-sharing platforms and the prominence of public-interest content.

Von der Leyen II Commission · part of the Competitiveness · One Europe, One Market agenda

Where it stands

Announced inEvaluation
Proposal expectedAfter the evaluation due 19 Dec 2026
Target for agreementnot set

Latest · October 2026

The evaluation required by Article 33 is due by 19 December 2026; a proposal may follow.

What it would do

  • Clarify whether influencers and professional creators fall under the Directive.
  • Stronger protection of minors on video-sharing platforms.
  • Prominence of general-interest content and access to major events.
  • Better fit with the DSA and the European Media Freedom Act.
