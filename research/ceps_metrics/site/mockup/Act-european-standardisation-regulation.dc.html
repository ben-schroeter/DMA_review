European Standardisation Regulation

Regulation (EU) 1025/2012 ↗Adopted 25 Oct 2012Evidence: thin

Proposed 1 Jun 2011 and adopted 25 Oct 2012 under the second Barroso Commission (Neelie Kroes) · Part of the Europe 2020 agenda

What it does

Sets the framework for cooperation between European standardisation organisations, national standards bodies, Member States and the Commission. It governs how the Commission requests European standards in support of EU law and policies, how ICT technical specifications can be identified for use in public procurement, how European standardisation is financed, and how SMEs and other stakeholders participate.

What we know so far

The Regulation promised faster European standards with more input from small firms and civil society. The Commission's 2025 evaluation found it removed conflicting national standards and made modest progress, but harmonised standards still take about six years to produce and smaller stakeholders have little real influence. No independent study measuring its economic effects was found.
