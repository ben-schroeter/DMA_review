Legal instruments / Media

European Media Freedom Act

Regulation (EU) 2024/1083 ↗Adopted 11 Apr 2024Evidence: thin

Proposed 16 Sep 2022 and adopted 11 Apr 2024 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Sets common rules for the internal market for media services while safeguarding media independence and pluralism. It protects editorial independence and journalistic sources, sets requirements for the independent functioning of public service media and for transparency of media ownership, and requires assessment of media market concentrations. It establishes the European Board for Media Services to support consistent application.

What we know so far

The Commission forecast that the Media Freedom Act would lift media companies' revenues by about EUR 2.9 billion a year and reduce risks to editorial independence. The Act has only applied in full since August 2025; the new EU media board is operating, but the Media Pluralism Monitor's latest results describe the situation before the Act and use a new scale. No study has yet measured its effects.
