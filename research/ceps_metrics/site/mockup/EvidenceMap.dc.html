What has been studied for each law

The 105 laws in force, grouped by policy area and ordered by the size of their rulebook, against eight kinds of effect. Of 840 pairs of law and effect, 312 have any evidence from after adoption and 54 have a causal study.

Each cell gives the number of sources and is coloured by the strongest one: dark gold, a causal study; light gold, another evaluation, audit, statistic or study; hatched, only the Commission's forecast; white, nothing found. Hover over a cell to see the sources, and find them in the library.

An empty cell means our searches found nothing, not that nothing exists. Counts are not quality, and one source can count for several effects. We sorted sources into effects by keywords on the outcomes recorded for each; a hand check of 150 found 86% tagged correctly before we fixed the commonest errors. Enforcement decisions and court rulings are not counted.
