Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform (STEP)

Regulation (EU) 2024/795 ↗Adopted 29 Feb 2024Evidence: too early to tell

Proposed 20 Jun 2023 and adopted 29 Feb 2024 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Establishes a platform to support the development and manufacturing of critical technologies in the digital, clean and biotech sectors and in defence, and their value chains. It channels funding from existing EU programmes, adds EUR 1.5 billion to the European Defence Fund envelope for STEP objectives, awards a 'Sovereignty Seal' to quality projects and creates a Sovereignty Portal listing funding opportunities.

What we know so far

The Commission claimed STEP could trigger up to EUR 160 billion of investment in critical technologies, but the final law added only EUR 1.5 billion of new money and mostly redirects existing EU funds. Its own interim review counts about EUR 15 billion steered to strategic technologies in the first year but no data yet on jobs, firms or competitiveness.
