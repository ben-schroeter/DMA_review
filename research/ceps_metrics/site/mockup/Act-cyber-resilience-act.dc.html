Legal instruments / Cybersecurity

Cyber Resilience Act

Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 ↗Adopted 23 Oct 2024Evidence: too early to tell

Proposed 15 Sep 2022 and adopted 23 Oct 2024 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Decade agenda

What it does

Aims to ensure the cybersecurity of hardware and software products with digital elements that connect to a device or network. It sets essential cybersecurity requirements for their design, development and production, and requires manufacturers to handle vulnerabilities throughout the period the products are expected to be in use. It sets obligations for manufacturers, importers and distributors and provides for market surveillance and enforcement.

What we know so far

The Commission forecast that mandatory security rules for connected products and software would cut the cost of cyber incidents for businesses by EUR 180-290 billion a year, against compliance costs of up to EUR 29 billion. The main requirements apply only from December 2027. An open-source industry survey in 2025 found low awareness and readiness, but no study has measured effects on security or costs.

Objectives and key results

Objectives are the aims the Commission set for the law. Key results are how it said it would measure them: indicators in the proposal and forecasts in the impact assessment, checked against the evidence available in October 2026.

Objectives

  • Coherent framework
  • Transparency and secure use
  • Fewer cyber incidents
