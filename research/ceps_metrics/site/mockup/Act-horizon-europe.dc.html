Horizon Europe

Regulation (EU) 2021/695 ↗Adopted 28 Apr 2021Evidence: moderate

Proposed 7 Jun 2018 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) and adopted 28 Apr 2021 under the first von der Leyen Commission (Margrethe Vestager, Thierry Breton) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Establishes the EU framework programme for research and innovation for 2021-2027 and its rules for participation and dissemination. It aims to strengthen the Union's scientific and technological bases, foster competitiveness, tackle global challenges and strengthen the European Research Area. It funds excellent science, collaborative research on global challenges and industrial competitiveness, and innovation through the European Innovation Council, with EUR 86.123 billion for its main specific programme and the EIT.

What we know so far

Horizon Europe, the EU's EUR 93.5 billion research programme, was forecast to return up to EUR 11 in GDP for every euro spent over 25 years and create up to 100,000 research jobs. Its 2025 interim evaluation says it is on track, but those figures come from re-running economic models; measured results so far are about 40,000 jobs reported by a fifth of projects and EUR 10 billion of co-investment. Rigorous studies of its predecessor find that grants helped small firms invest, patent and grow, while European Research Council grants made little difference to researchers just above the funding line.
