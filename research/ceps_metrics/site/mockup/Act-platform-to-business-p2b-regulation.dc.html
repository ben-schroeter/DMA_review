Platform-to-Business (P2B) Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 ↗Adopted 20 Jun 2019Evidence: thin

Proposed 26 Apr 2018 and adopted 20 Jun 2019 under the Juncker Commission (Andrus Ansip) · Part of the Digital Single Market agenda

What it does

Aims to ensure that business users of online intermediation services, and corporate website users of online search engines, get appropriate transparency, fairness and effective redress. It applies to providers serving EU-based businesses that offer goods or services to EU consumers, and requires clear terms and conditions, notice before restrictions or termination, disclosure of ranking parameters and differentiated treatment, internal complaint-handling systems and mediation.

What we know so far

The P2B Regulation (applying since July 2020) gave businesses that sell through platforms rights to clear terms, ranking explanations and complaint handling. The Commission forecast EUR 0.8-4 billion of extra online trade but never measured it; its 2023 review and later national checks found low compliance (around 80% of platforms in an Irish 2025 sweep) and low awareness among businesses. In November 2025 the Commission proposed repealing it as largely overtaken by the DSA and DMA.
