MeasuredOutcome data show whether the objective was met. Example: connectivity targets under the Electronic Communications Code.
Partly measuredSome parts or years are measured, others not. Example: Single Digital Gateway procedures online.
Mixed evidenceStudies exist but point in different directions. Example: whether InvestEU support is additional.
Activity onlyOnly outputs are reported (inspections, decisions, funds committed), not whether the objective was achieved. Example: DMA enforcement, with no per-gatekeeper outcome series.
Never measuredWe found no data on it. Example: the cut in administrative burden expected from the GDPR.

Of 68 studies with a causal design, more than half cover five instruments. Most of the rulebook, including almost every act adopted since 2023, has never been studied this way.

Measured and kept Roaming Regulation Several peer-reviewed causal studies of "roam like at home", plus Commission reviews in 2019 and 2025.
Forecast, not yet testable Data Act The Commission forecast €270bn of extra GDP by 2028. The act has applied only since September 2025.
Causal studies, no indicators Digital Markets Act Eight causal studies: choice screens shifted browser and search use a little; Google's redesigned results page sent more traffic to airlines and smaller comparison sites; rival map services saw no gain. None of the six indicators set in the proposal is reported.

How we graded

StrongSeveral independent causal studies with consistent results, plus an official evaluation.
ModerateAt least one causal or robust quantitative study, or a solid evaluation with measured outcomes.
ThinDescriptive evidence, activity counts or qualitative reviews only.
Forecasts onlyAdopted 2024–26 or still pending: impact assessment forecasts, nothing yet to measure.
What counts as evidenceEvaluations, audits, statistics, research and think tank pieces that assess what happened, each labelled by who produced it. Initial impact assessments are shown with the proposal's objectives; enforcement decisions and court rulings are not counted.
"Not measured"Not found by our searches. It is not proof that no study exists.
