Administrative cooperation in taxation (DAC)

Directive 2011/16/EU ↗Adopted 15 Feb 2011Evidence: moderate

Proposed 2 Feb 2009 under the first Barroso Commission (Viviane Reding) and adopted 15 Feb 2011 under the second Barroso Commission (Neelie Kroes) · Part of the Lisbon agenda

What it does

Lays down rules and procedures for Member States to cooperate in exchanging information foreseeably relevant to administering and enforcing their tax laws. It covers taxes of all kinds except VAT, customs and excise duties, and compulsory social contributions. It provides for exchange on request, automatic and spontaneous exchange, simultaneous controls and administrative enquiries, using electronic means.

What we know so far

The EU's tax-information exchange rules set no monitoring indicators. The Commission's 2025 evaluation estimates they raise about EUR 6.8 billion a year in tax against running costs of about EUR 646 million, mostly paid by business, though EU auditors found much exchanged data is poor quality or unused. Independent research on automatic bank-account exchange finds an 11.5% drop in deposits in tax havens, with some money moving to non-participating countries.
