Why we built it

[Your text: who is behind the project and why. Two or three short paragraphs.]

Europe debates whether it regulates technology too much, too little or simply badly. That debate rarely has numbers behind it. This site counts the rules in EU digital law, follows them into implementing acts and guidance, and checks what is known about their effects.
