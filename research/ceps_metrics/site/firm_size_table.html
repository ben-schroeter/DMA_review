Size bandDefinitionWhere the laws draw the line
MicroUnder 10 staff and turnover or balance sheet up to €2 million (EU SME definition, Recommendation 2003/361)DORA: some ICT-risk duties apply only to financial firms other than micro. EHDS: health-data-holder duties start above micro.
Small10–49 staff, turnover or balance sheet up to €10 millionExempt up to here: DSA platform and marketplace duties and transparency reports (Arts 15, 19, 29), P2B complaint handling and mediation, Data Act data-sharing chapter, Ecodesign rules on unsold products.
Medium50–249 staff, turnover up to €50 million or balance sheet up to €43 millionNIS2 cybersecurity duties apply from here (telecoms providers at any size). The GDPR record-keeping exemption ends at 250 staff, but its conditions make it rarely usable, so it is not counted.
Small mid-cap250–749 staff, turnover up to €150 million (category proposed in 2025)No adopted law in the rulebook gives this band any relief. The 2025 simplification proposals would extend some SME exemptions to it.
Large750 staff or moreTurnover thresholds: EU Merger Regulation (€5 billion worldwide and €250 million EU turnover, or the lower alternative), Foreign Subsidies Regulation (€500 million EU turnover). Critical Raw Materials Act risk preparedness for firms over 500 staff and €150 million.
DesignatedNamed by the Commission: gatekeepers (€7.5 billion EU turnover or €75 billion market value, 45 million users); very large online platforms and search engines (45 million EU users); general-purpose AI with systemic riskDigital Markets Act; DSA Arts 33–43 and the audit and reporting acts under them; AI Act Art 55.
