How much does Europe regulate tech, and does it work?

A new open dataset counts the legal obligations in every EU digital law, follows them into the implementing acts and guidance behind them, and checks what is known about their effects.

A first look

Three findings from the data

×4.2

EU tech rules aimed directly at companies have quadrupled since 2018: from 1,685 obligations at the start of 2018 to 7,106 today, in the laws themselves.

84 of 99

EU digital laws came out of negotiation in Parliament and Council with more obligations than the Commission proposed, 26% more in total.

55 of 105

adopted EU digital laws date from 2020 or later. More than half of today's digital rulebook is less than seven years old.
